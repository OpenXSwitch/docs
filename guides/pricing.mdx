## Pricing FAQ

### Wallet Usage & Metering Model

OpenXSwitch tracks wallet utilization based on provisioned wallets (wallets created, regardless of whether they are active or idle) to ensure fair resource allocation.

### **How Wallet Provisioning Works**

- **Creation-Based Metering:** OpenXSwitch charges based on wallets created within your workspace—we do not bill based on monthly active wallet status.
- **Multi-Chain Address Allocation Example:**
  - EVM chains share a single unified address.
  - Solana and Bitcoin issue distinct single-chain addresses.
  - **User Capacity Calculation:** If your application provisions 1 EVM address, 1 Solana address, and 1 Bitcoin address per user (3 total wallet addresses), the **2,500 wallet allowance** in the Basic Plan allows you to fully serve **~833 active end-users** across all three ecosystems without overage.

- **Fixed Wallet Package Guarantee:** Clients on a One-Time Setup Package incur **no wallet overage fees**; your provisioned wallets remain valid within your purchased capacity threshold.

#### **How Volume Overages Work**

- Transfer volume is calculated based on the total USD-equivalent value of outgoing on-chain transfers and withdrawals across all supported networks.
- The Basic Plan includes **\$100,000 in outgoing volume** each month. Outgoing transfer volume exceeding \$100,000 is billed at an overage rate of **0.20%**.
